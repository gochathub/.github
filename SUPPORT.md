# Getting help

## Free community support

- **Docs:** the [install guide](https://gochathub.com/install/) and each repository's README.
- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue in the repository concerned (bug and feature forms are provided). Community help is best effort, with no response-time promise.
- **Security problems:** do not open an issue. See the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Paid support

Paid installation, Rocket.Chat migration and maintenance plans are available. Scope, prices and terms are on [gochathub.com/support](https://gochathub.com/support/); to ask a question, use [gochathub.com/contact](https://gochathub.com/contact/).
