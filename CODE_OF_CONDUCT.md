# Code of conduct

goChatHub is a small open-source project. Be respectful and keep discussion
about the work.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming otherwise.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block repeat offenders from the organization's
repositories.

To report a problem, email info@gochathub.com, use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the comment, or message a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. Security vulnerabilities go through the [security policy](SECURITY.md)
instead.
